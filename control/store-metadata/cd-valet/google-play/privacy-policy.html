CD Valet Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-04-29

CD Valet helps music collectors catalog personal CD libraries. The app is local-first and does not require an account.

Data we collect

  • Local library data: UPCs you scan, manually entered CDs, album titles, artists, formats, and notes stored on your device.
  • Optional profile data: If you choose to save a local profile, the email address you enter is stored on your device.
  • Camera access: Used only when you choose to scan CD UPC barcodes.
  • Barcode lookup requests: When you scan a UPC, CD Valet may send that UPC/barcode value to MusicBrainz to retrieve public album metadata.
  • Diagnostics: CD Valet does not include third-party advertising SDKs. Basic platform logs may exist on your device to help troubleshoot issues, but TixPy does not collect analytics or advertising data from the app.

How we use data

  • Provide CD Valet's core library, scanning, manual entry, and editing functionality.
  • Retrieve album metadata from MusicBrainz after a UPC scan.
  • Improve performance and fix bugs.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. CD Valet library records and optional profile data stay on your device unless a future version adds sync features with clear notice. UPC/barcode values may be sent to MusicBrainz only to perform album metadata lookup.

Data retention

Local library data and optional local profile data remain on your device unless you delete the app or clear data.

Account deletion

CD Valet does not require an account. For help removing local profile or library data, email TixPy@pinataro.com with the subject CD Valet Data Deletion Help.
