CD Valet helps music collectors build a personal CD library with quick barcode scanning, manual entry, and simple local album notes.

Scan UPCs when you have the case in hand, or add a CD manually when a barcode is missing. Each saved item can hold album title, artist, format, and notes, so your collection stays easy to review later.

CD Valet is local-first and works without requiring an account.
